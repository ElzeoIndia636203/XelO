For programming labs in Indian engineering colleges

Every lab, planned, run and reported without the paperwork.

XeIO is an AI faculty assistant. It drafts the session plan, the questions and the test banks; you approve them. Students code in a real workspace, the end-sem is proctored, and the course file writes itself as the semester runs.

One path through the semester

  1. PlanCourse outcomes with Bloom's levels, CO–PO map and rubric. Your HOD approves once.
  2. PrepareThirty sessions from the academic calendar. Assist drafts four questions per session; you pick two.
  3. RunAttendance opens the lab and closes the pre-test. Watch every student's progress live.
  4. AssessForty new end-sem questions per batch, thirty approved, sent to the HOD, then proctored.
  5. ReportMark lists, CO attainment, bell curves and a fourteen-section course file, ready for NBA and NAAC.

For faculty

Assist drafts. You decide.

Nothing reaches a student until you approve it. Questions are checked by running their reference solutions against every test before you can even select them, and banks keep pre-lab and post-lab items apart.

When the semester ends, the work is already done: internal marks follow the rubric your HOD approved, attainment is computed per CO, and slow and fast learners are named with reasons.

Integrity and privacy

Built for audits, not just demos.

  • Sign-in only with institutional accounts that were invited; no self-signup.
  • Students can start the end-sem only after being marked present and the faculty activates the test.
  • Fullscreen, camera and screen checks; copy, paste and AI tools blocked; events logged for the examiner, never auto-penalised.
  • Hidden tests never leave the server. Code runs in an isolated sandbox.
  • Every approval and mark change is recorded in an append-only audit log.
  • Student data stays with your institution's database, in line with the DPDP Act.

Questions colleges ask

Does AI decide marks?

No. Marks come only from attendance, test results and examiner entries. AI drafts questions, banks and summaries; faculty approve everything first.

Who approves what?

Faculty approve questions and banks. HODs approve course plans, end-sem sets and final marks. The CoE accepts HOD-approved internals into results.

How do students join?

Faculty import the section roster; students receive an invitation with their batch's class code and sign in with their institutional account.
